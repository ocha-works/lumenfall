LUMENFALL is an arena survival game where a glowing paper-lantern spirit keeps watch over a night garden washed in deep indigo, driving back waves of ink shades — drifting moths and blobs, and a Great Moth boss every fifth wave. Gather fallen light motes to mend your flame and grab embers for a burst of rapid fire, and see how many waves your light can outlast.

## How to play

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys
- **Aim:** mouse
- **Fire:** hold the mouse button or Space to shoot light motes
- **Dash:** Shift (brief burst with a light trail, on a cooldown)
- **Pause:** P
- **Mute:** M

Survive each wave, collect the light motes shades drop to restore health, and pick up embers for temporary rapid fire. When your health runs out, the results screen shows your score and waves survived — press R or click to start a new watch.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a browser
```
